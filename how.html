How It Works ⚡

Learn how PowCal helps you estimate and understand the power consumption of your household and office appliances.

What is PowCal?

PowCal is a simple power consumption calculator designed to help users estimate the amount of electricity used by common appliances. Whether you want to understand your energy usage, manage electricity costs, or compare appliances, PowCal provides quick and easy calculations based on average power ratings.

How Does It Work?

  • Step 1: Select an appliance from the available list.
  • Step 2: Enter the number of hours the appliance is used per day.
  • Step 3: PowCal uses the average power rating of the appliance to estimate energy consumption.
  • Step 4: View the estimated electricity usage and compare it with other appliances.

Understanding the Calculation

Electricity consumption is usually measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh). PowCal estimates energy usage using the appliance's average power rating and the number of hours it is used.

Energy Consumption (Wh) = Power (W) × Hours Used

For example, if a 75-watt ceiling fan runs for 8 hours:

75 W × 8 h = 600 Wh (0.6 kWh)

Why Use PowCal?

Understanding how much electricity your appliances consume can help you make smarter energy decisions, reduce unnecessary usage, and estimate your electricity expenses more accurately. PowCal makes these calculations easy and accessible for everyone.
