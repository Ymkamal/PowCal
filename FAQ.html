Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Find answers to common questions about electricity, power consumption, and how PowCal works.

What is PowCal?

PowCal is an online tool that helps users estimate the power consumption of household and office appliances using their average power ratings and usage hours.

What is a Watt (W)?

A watt is a unit of power. It measures how much electrical power an appliance uses at a given moment.

What is a Kilowatt (kW)?

One kilowatt equals 1,000 watts. Larger appliances and electricity bills are often measured in kilowatts.

What is a Kilowatt-hour (kWh)?

A kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy. It represents the amount of electricity used when a 1-kilowatt appliance runs for one hour.

How does PowCal calculate energy consumption?

PowCal uses the appliance's average power rating and the number of hours it is used.

Formula:
Energy Consumption (Wh) = Power (W) × Hours Used

Are the calculations 100% accurate?

PowCal provides estimates based on average power ratings. Actual consumption may vary depending on appliance model, condition, and usage patterns.

Why do some appliances consume more power than others?

Appliances that generate heat, cooling, or mechanical movement—such as air conditioners, electric irons, and water heaters—typically consume more electricity than devices like LED bulbs or phone chargers.

Can PowCal help reduce my electricity bill?

Yes. By understanding which appliances consume the most electricity, you can make informed decisions to reduce energy usage and potentially lower your electricity costs.

Why are appliance power ratings different?

Different brands and models are designed differently. As a result, power consumption can vary even among appliances that perform the same function.

Who can use PowCal?

Anyone can use PowCal—homeowners, students, tenants, businesses, and anyone interested in understanding electricity consumption.
