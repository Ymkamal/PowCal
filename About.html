About PowCal ⚡

Making electricity consumption simple and easy to understand.

What is PowCal?

PowCal is a simple and user-friendly power consumption calculator designed to help users estimate and understand the amount of electricity their appliances consume.

The platform allows users to select an appliance, provide its power rating, enter the number of hours it is used, and calculate its estimated energy consumption in Watt-hours (Wh).

PowCal was designed to make power consumption calculations easier and more accessible without requiring users to perform complicated calculations manually.

Why I Created PowCal

Electricity is an important part of everyday life, but understanding how much energy different appliances use is not always straightforward.

I created PowCal to provide a simple way for people to estimate their energy consumption and gain a better understanding of how their everyday appliances contribute to their overall electricity usage.

The project combines my interest in electrical engineering, energy management, and technology to create something practical and useful.
