Power Consumption Calculator

Welcome to PowCal, your simple and reliable power consumption calculator. This platform is designed to help you estimate and understand the electricity usage of different household, office, and industrial appliances. Whether you want to check the power rating of a single device or calculate your total daily energy consumption, PowCal makes it easy. Simply select your appliances, enter their usage details, and instantly view their power consumption in Watts (W) or Kilowatts (kW). Our goal is to help you make informed decisions about energy use, improve efficiency, and better manage your electricity costs.
